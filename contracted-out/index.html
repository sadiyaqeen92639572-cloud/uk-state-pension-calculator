What Is COPE?

COPE stands for Contracted Out Pension Equivalent. It appears on many people's UK state pension forecast as a deduction applied when working out their "starting amount" under the old pre-2016 pension rules. It represents the additional State Pension (State Second Pension / SERPS) you would have built up for years you were contracted out — years when you and your employer paid a lower rate of National Insurance because you were instead paying into a workplace, personal, or stakeholder pension.

Your Money Isn't Lost

COPE is shown for information, not as a penalty. The money that would otherwise have gone into the additional State Pension went into your other pension scheme instead — so in principle it should already be reflected in that scheme's value. This is why your state pension "starting amount" can be lower than the maximum even with 35+ qualifying years — the shortfall corresponds to a benefit you're expected to receive elsewhere.

Contracting Out Timeline

PeriodWhat happened
1978–1988Contracting out only via employer defined benefit (final salary) schemes.
1988–2012Personal and stakeholder pensions could also be used to contract out.
2012–2016Contracting out via defined contribution schemes ended (2012); only DB schemes remained.
6 April 2016Contracting out abolished entirely with the new State Pension. Everyone now builds the same single-tier pension.

Why We Don't Offer a Precise Calculator

Some sites present a "COPE calculator" that outputs a specific weekly figure from just your birth year and salary. That figure cannot be accurate — COPE depends on exactly which years you were contracted out, which type of scheme, contracted-out contribution rates at the time (these changed repeatedly), and any subsequent scheme transfers. Presenting an invented precise number risks people making retirement decisions on a figure that has no real basis. Our approach: explain the mechanism clearly, give a wide indicative range for orientation only, and point directly to the one source with your real data.
